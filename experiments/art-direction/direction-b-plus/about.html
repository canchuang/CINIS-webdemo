Design Philosophy

Structure first. Before style, I want the information architecture to make sense — what the user came for, and the shortest honest path to it.

Type carries the argument. Hierarchy, scale and rhythm do more persuasive work than decoration. Whitespace is a material, not empty space.

Motion serves narrative. Movement should clarify state and pace a story — never decorate a page that hasn't earned it.

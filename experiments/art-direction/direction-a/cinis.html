CINIS

ROLE UI/UX · Product Design TYPE Gamified self-discipline product SCOPE IA · Flow · Interaction · UI System YEAR 2026

01 — Overview

What & why

CINIS turns habit-building into a role-playing journey. Instead of tracking streaks, users commit to daily tasks, accumulate 魂火 (soul-flame), and face staged consequences across three distinct modes — making discipline feel like a story worth continuing.

02 — Background & Problem

The tension

Most habit apps optimise for streaks and guilt: miss a day and the number resets, the motivation dies. The design problem was to build consequence without shame — a system where failure is part of the narrative, not a punishment that ends it.

DESIGN GOAL

Reframe discipline as a game you want to keep playing — three difficulty modes, a visible growth system, and failure states that invite return rather than withdrawal.

03 — Three-Mode Mechanism

旅者 / 骑士 / 深渊

Traveler

旅者 · 无门槛

Low-stakes onboarding. Forgiving failure — restart from a small flame. Best for building the first habit.

Knight

骑士 · 誓约警戒

Commitment with a recoverable stake. Fail and lose progress that can be reclaimed — tension without despair.

Abyss

深渊 · 契约已断

Hard mode. High reward, harsh reset. The gamble that makes every day feel consequential.

Each mode has its own failure branch with independent structure and visuals — not a simple recolour.

04 — 魂火 Growth System

Progress as narrative

Soul-flame (魂火) is the core currency: it rises with completed tasks, powers stages, and visualises momentum. Milestone stones mark each stage — progress you can literally see on the path.
